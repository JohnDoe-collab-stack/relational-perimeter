# Independent design check: positive formation and deployment

Referee `/root/positive_foundation_referee`, lot 2 of the positive foundations plan, 2026-10-09. New archive only; prior referee reports preserved. Read plan §5 and the current primitive, positive-presentation, and unpointed-boundary interfaces. Labyrinth independent review method applies. No compilation before readiness, canonical edit, full build, commit, publication, or delegation.

The proposed `PositiveFormation` with primitive data, `State`, a complete node readout, a typed family `Step`, and step compatibility is an adequate minimal positive path interface. It receives that signature and its interpretations. It does not create the primitive sorts, prove freshness of differences/provenances, or guarantee an available successor from every state. A chosen continuation interface, if supplied, is distinct from the admissible step family and does not establish determinism of all steps.

Free finite histories should preserve the supplied State/Step indices and deploy recursively to witnessed spines. The initial node must be the readout of the source state; the final node must equal the readout of the target state. Appending deployments must use that terminal agreement to reindex the second spine explicitly. Nil/unit and associativity agreements should be constructive, with no global equality of arbitrary functions needed.

Occurrence positions over a fixed history can have exact constructor-by-constructor transport to nonclosing positions of its deployment. Empty history must have neither occurrences nor positions, and any positive constructor supplies a concrete occurrence. Equality of raw node values must not identify distinct `here` and `later here` positions.

The preservation scope is crucial: the spine stores `compatibility(step)`, not an arbitrary full `Step` record. Distinct Step values may project to the same compatibility and produce the same spine. The occurrence/position transport is indexed by the supplied history and does not make deployment injective on all histories. An independent model with Step Bool and constant Unit compatibility will test this explicitly.

The closing shape can be read from source and final target state/node data before a junction is supplied. A history occurrence and a concrete closing witness then construct a positive circular presentation. A positive path need not inhabit the closing fibre; the empty-fibre model must therefore have usable forward compatibility and unavailable backward/closing compatibility. Adding endpoint readings and obstruction afterwards should use the historical bridge in a separate import layer.

The planned four models are suitable: forward path with empty closing fibre, a pointed closing path, two distinct admissible successors of the same source, and a repeated raw node with two distinct history occurrences. Required source review will also check actual step compatibility readout, endpoint and provenance views, append with an indexed suffix, historical bridge returns, and source comments limited to this path/deployment interface. General formation semantics, history-signature transports, full role classification, and rigid determination of arbitrary Step data are outside lot 2.
