## Complete relation families

The primitive signature must retain the explicit, implicit and difference carriers and the compatibility/provenance Type families. Exact maps of the three sorts alone do not imply any relation-fibre transport. A full fibre interface should give explicit reversible maps for every compatibility source/target and every provenance difference, with target indices carried by those sort maps.

The inverse calculation is essential: for target indices i/e, transporting backward into the source fibre uses the source indices backward(i)/backward(e); that forward fibre lands at forward(backward(i))/forward(backward(e)). The target witness must be reindexed along both carrier return laws before the fibre's backward map can consume it. Provenance has the analogous difference reindexing. The calculation must be explicit and constructive.

Composition should instantiate the second fibre transport at the images of the first's indices, compose its maps, and prove the round trips. Pointwise unit and associativity need to be statements about actual fibre maps; inverse fibre laws must expose any casts from backward(forward(index)) back to the original indices. Global equalities of functions or transport structures are not needed. If double inverse or reverse-composition laws are claimed, they need their dependent fibre reindexing as well.

Restriction to selected boundary transport additionally needs source, target, difference and both chosen-witness agreements. Full-family equivalence cannot manufacture preservation of a chosen junction or provenance. The resulting selected fibre maps should be exactly the family maps followed by the required index-equality transports, with their returns.

Required separators: identical sort carriers but one incompatible inhabited/empty selected fibre (no full-family transport along specified carrier maps); exact full-family witness permutations failing specified chosen-junction preservation; a separate provenance permutation preserving junction but failing chosen provenance. The obstruction concerns the specified maps, not existence of all possible maps between those signatures/boundaries.

## Formation, nodes and paths

Node transport must map complete primitive data, including selected provenance and internal compatibility at transported indices. A formation transport must specify how State and Step data relate beyond the primitive signature. If it supplies only forward maps on State/Step, it proves a unidirectional history map; reversible preservation of full State/Step payloads requires corresponding exact maps/returns. A compatibility readout agreement alone cannot restore arbitrary extra Step metadata.

The node readout can be noninjective: distinct States may read the same LocalNode. Transport must not silently identify them or infer uniqueness of all states/successors. Full Step fibres may contain additional metadata; its preservation must match exactly what the implemented Step interface supplies.

Mapping paths by nil/cons should preserve typed endpoint indices, append, and deployment, with explicit transport along node readout agreements. A mapped spine should preserve its node reads and each compatibility witness. Positions should map by matching constructors. Structural precedence and immediate successor require their own preservation/reflection proofs; an exact carrier correspondence alone does not preserve these relations.

Required independent probes will exercise nonidentity sort maps and genuinely index-dependent fibres, reindexed inversion/composition, independently mismatched relation fibres, both distinguished-witness swap failures, noninjective node readouts, extra Step metadata, nontrivial appended histories, positions, precedence and immediate-next. Generic positive histories may cycle in state; canonical historical non-return is not imported as a general transport property.

Endpoint pole maps, contraction obstruction, complete circular role grammar and constitutive quantity remain separate unless their own transport agreements are supplied. Formal verification and a small `.lean.in` compilation await the coordinator's readiness signal; no full build will be run by this referee.
