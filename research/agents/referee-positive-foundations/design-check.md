## Proposed split

The historical presentation has nine positive fields followed by endpoint and obstruction fields. Extracting the spine primitives without changing their namespace, constructors, or defining equations should preserve existing consumers. A separate positive presentation removes the logical implication of endpoint separation from the definition of circularity. The positive parent remains a *nonempty finite perimeter* presentation because `perimeterPositive` supplies a nonclosing position; this is a precise intended restriction, not arbitrary circular architecture.

`EndpointBoundary` must admit a common-pole model. `CircularClosureObstruction` may then supply the contraction rejection. The historical structure can extend the positive parent while retaining its remaining endpoint/loop fields. Exact forward/back adapters should expose reconstruction laws for all data, not merely existential correspondence.

## Selected boundary transport

The requested boundary contains selected source, target, difference, closing fibre, provenance fibre, and their distinguished witnesses. Exact maps on the carriers and selected fibres are adequate for this scope when accompanied by source, target, and difference agreement and independent equations preserving both distinguished witnesses. Reversing a transport must derive those equations using the round trips, rather than assume extra inverse preservation. Composition must explicitly calculate preservation of the selected witnesses. The intended pointwise unit, associative, and inverse laws are sufficient; equality of function-containing structures is unnecessary.

This interface does not by itself preserve the full compatibility or provenance families over arbitrary indices, endpoint pole operations, perimeter order/adjacency, generation, realization, or history composition. It therefore closes a selected boundary subproblem of §§11 and 17, not the entire general constitutive quantity transport programme. Source/target maps still permit comparison across heterogeneous carrier types.

## Final role and required countermodels

The final role fibre can remain contractible. Boundary information must live in its index or equipped total object and be read authoritatively from that boundary (or carried with exact agreement). A uniform fibre equivalence to `Unit` remains possible and does not erase the equipped index automatically. Equality of equipped totals should project equality of boundaries; exact rich transport should instead transport their selected data with the stated agreements.

Required checks after implementation:

1. Positive circle and common endpoint model, and a proof that the same common endpoint boundary cannot carry the rejection enrichment.
2. Historical obstructed example adaptation and exact adapter round trips.
3. A selected `Bool` closing fibre with distinguished witness `false`, whose bare exact negation map sends that witness to `true`; show that rich transport with that specified fibre map is impossible. This refutes automatic preservation, not all rich transport between the underlying boundaries.
4. Prefer an analogous provenance swap probe to confirm that provenance preservation is independent of closing-witness preservation.
5. Distinct boundaries with contractible final role fibres, to retain the distinction between fibre and equipped total.
