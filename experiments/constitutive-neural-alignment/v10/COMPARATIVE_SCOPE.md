# Pre-run comparative scope and residual-gain rule

This document fixes the interpretation of the v10 capability comparison before
its reserved confirmatory execution.  It is not a result record and it does
not replace the immutable raw output.

## What is compared

Nine repository-local computations consume one exact frozen material package:
six typed class adapters, the relational execution comparison, the explicit
reference combination and the independent complete recheck.  Each method
receives every frozen input named by its interface.  The translation ledger
counts those explicit conversions, and `method-correspondence.json` records
the property, domain, arithmetic, version and trust boundary actually used.

The six class adapters are representatives implemented in this repository.
Their results do not establish a theorem about every external system in the
corresponding research class.  Exact comparison with external implementations
and exact-passage literature claims remain open under S1 and S2.

## Answers required by the comparative protocol

1. **Additional capability on the same trace.**  The local relational method
   jointly observes equal current tables, a nonempty common continuation,
   different future outcomes and opposite future-contract satisfaction.  The
   constitutive runtime additionally accepts the aligned update and refuses
   the bypass before installing its effect.  These are local formal results;
   they are not yet a residual novelty result.
2. **Capabilities already covered by the local reference rows.**  The
   interventional adapter detects the authorization dependency; shielding and
   the explicit reference combination allow the aligned candidate and refuse
   the bypass; the self-adaptive runtime installs the aligned transition; the
   incremental checker and independent full recheck agree on acceptance and
   the produced successor; the deployment adapter propagates its checked
   contract along the installed history.
3. **Difference remaining after those successes.**  No unique discriminatory
   advantage is currently certified for P1: the explicit reference
   combination and the independent full recheck also distinguish the two
   candidates.  What remains locally formalized is the conjunction of the
   dependencies, executed productions, contract transitions, licensed reuse,
   future divergence and forgetting on one typed lineage.  Whether this
   conjunction is absent from prior constructions is not established by v10.
4. **Source of the result.**  The local result is tied to the typed
   constitutive dependencies and to direct computations.  It is not inferred
   from a method name, from current-table indistinguishability or from a
   missing adapter.  An out-of-interface or unavailable comparison remains
   open and never becomes an impossibility statement.
5. **Cost and practical limits.**  V10 counts interface translations only.
   Those counts are bookkeeping, not runtime cost.  Scaling, uncertainty,
   memory, complete-recheck cost and reuse margins belong to the separately
   frozen P8/S6 protocol and cannot be inferred from this comparison.

## Residual-gain decision

V10 may report the exact nine method outputs and the exact P1--P7 cell
statuses.  It may not declare a residual scientific gain while any S1--S5,
S7 or S8 criterion is `not-certified`.  In particular, a successful local
run will establish that the comparison was executed reproducibly; it will not
by itself establish literature novelty, external-class equivalence or a
universal limitation of another method family.
