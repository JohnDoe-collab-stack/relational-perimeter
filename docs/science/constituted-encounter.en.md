# Constituted encounter: admission, local description and path effects

Two receptions preserved in a history do not authorize a new interaction.
The added local model separates that archive from present availability:
both ports must have received their data, and their availability is consumed
by the joint interaction. The interaction produces a fresh occurrence
without replacing either arrival occurrence.

Both participants then describe the same produced encounter. That agreement
does not identify their sources or their paths. In the constructed example,
the readings agree but one path records two increments and the other none.
The same attached-effects request still distinguishes their rich descriptions
after any finite continuation.

## Declared law and scope

This is an **ideal local two-port coupling model with retention**. The
instrument is attached to a calibration occurrence in the actual received
constituted prefix. Delivery reads an available signal reference on that
support and produces a reception at a vacant port. The first port retains
its reception until joint consumption. Other signal operations transport
it. Occupied ports reject delivery, and interaction without both occupied
ports is refused.

The law is a declared input, not discovered or empirically validated physics.
Signals are **reusable local records**, not linear particles for which every
copy is forbidden. New delivery from an old record produces a new reception;
it does not renew permission to consume the old reception. Admission receives
no propagation speed, distance, metric, global simultaneity or coordinate.

The numerical interaction response reuses the existing calibrated comparison.
Its delivery formation and port consumption distinguish it from an archive
comparison. These ports belong to the declared law; they are not yet a
network of physical locations.

## Constructed chain

```text
received constituted prefix and calibration
  -> availability formed by delivery productions
  -> positive admission or refusal
  -> one joint production and its successor
  -> two presentations of this produced encounter
  -> LocationAgreement and common local interaction occurrence
  -> actual successor resumption and attached-effect readings
```

`State` requires `CouplingFormation`, whose transitions carry actual executed
reception productions. `EncounterAdmission` reconstructs the two available
arrivals; it does not receive `LocationAgreement`. `performEncounter` produces
the comparison from these references. `afterEncounter` consumes that same
production and clears both ports.

`LocalizedPresentation` selects a participant of this production and a
constituted description path. Its localizing occurrence is the executed
interaction, not a supplied point or constant numerical response.
`producedLocationAgreement` connects these two participations in that exact
encounter. Its consumer `LocationAgreement.site` transports their references
through an exact raccord. This is **not yet a location in the reconstructed
relativistic continuum**.

Exact description changes have return laws for occurrences and effects.
Composition requires the same intermediate presentation. Extensions follow
stored productions and preserve the same past encounter; they do not colocate
every future participant event. Two new interactions at the same instrument
remain distinct occurrences even when their responses agree.

## Contracts and observable separation

`contract` permits arbitrary finite interleavings of emission, relay,
reception, typed inspection, delivery and interaction, including refusals.
`run` binds one response per request and resumes its actual produced state.
`all_futures_exact` and `final_state_exact` connect this runner with the
specification. `run_append_state` and `history_transport_append` connect
successive resumptions and their references.

Old inspections are translated through the actual extension references,
with `transported_inspection_exact`. Their local addresses can change;
they are not coordinates. `StateRaccord` also carries the instrument and exact
occupied phase of two already positively formed states. It translates the
entire grammar between those descriptions: local actions, deliveries,
interactions and inspections, preserving both admissions and refusals.
After a production, the raccord evolves with the two actual successors.
`runSharedCoupling` produces one source response and transports its stored
role and output to the other presentation. `transported_all_futures` proves
exactness for every finite list, without filtering refusals. The closed
client exchanges two cached determinations without rerunning them, and shows
that freezing the initial address permutation would break the contract.

`readerContract` separately fixes rich-description requests. The same
`attachedEffects` request reads each participant's own attached occurrence.
`rich_futures_require_effects` proves that future-equivalent descriptions
have equal effects. The closed `Example.same_request_forbids_rich_grouping`
refutes that equivalence for the two paths after any principal-contract
request list. This is not merely a comparison of different address lists.

These local contracts replace neither the master's contract nor the future
full physical contract. Localizing agreement alone licenses no forgetting.

## Encounter passages and reading constraints

Temporal succession alone does not establish a causal connection between
encounters. `producePassageHeads` binds an emission from the preceding
encounter output, two new deliveries of the emitted record, and their admitted
interaction. `produceLinkedEncounter` consumes that stored packet and builds
a positive path of actually used ports to the new occurrence. The two
receptions remain distinct even when reading the same record. `PassageCourse`
composes these links and histories on their actual produced successors. Its
executable extension accepts any finite length; the single-link producer
receives no future horizon. `PassageCourse.used` supplies the positive
endpoint dependency of every nonempty course.

The used path proves that these occurrences are distinct. Each encounter
has its own local participant agreement. The prior agreement and attached
effects are carried forward, not extended to an agreement identifying the
passage endpoints. Their numerical values nevertheless agree in the closed
client. This is a causal encounter course on one constituted instrument,
not yet a network of independent physical instruments, a distance or a
propagation law.

`EncounterReadingConstraints` connects those presentations to existing finite
reading constraints and covers. Encounter agreement carries clauses on its
actual interaction reading, and a cover selects leaves from the transported
certificates. Restriction returns input readings; refinement erases no attached
effect. Rich clauses follow exact reexpression of the same participant or the
continuation history, not the localizing agreement alone between participants.
The corresponding expected-failure fixture rejects that substitution. These
remain reading covers, not physical neighborhoods or relativistic coverage.

## Evidence and checks

The [French declaration table](rencontre-constituee.fr.md#preuves-et-contrôles)
maps every passage to its sources. The public client is
[EncounterChecks](../../Tests/Relativity/EncounterChecks.lean).
[check-encounter-codegen.py](../../scripts/check-encounter-codegen.py) checks
named producers, helpers, branches and description-only transport without
producer replay.
The additional public clients are [TransportedEncounterChecks](../../Tests/Relativity/TransportedEncounterChecks.lean)
and [LocatedEncounterChecks](../../Tests/Relativity/LocatedEncounterChecks.lean):
a nonidentity permutation, the full contract, anchor-reading constraints and
arbitrary finite actual used courses.

This compiled check bounds applications at those declared local boundaries,
not internal producer work, total heap usage or physical cost. `run` and
`encounterThen` are the shared executable path; generic contract evaluation
uses separate specification projections.
The check also covers the shared two-presentation response and the passage
packet's four producers: one emission, two deliveries and one interaction.
It separates this counted packet boundary from its descriptive consumer and
checks absence of statically reachable producers outside that boundary.
It does not measure formation-witness or transport costs.
