\paragraph{Status.}
This document summarizes, without extending, the registered claim
\texttt{GODEL\_CONSTITUTIVE\_THEORY\_PROGRESSION}. The canonical passage
remains the English Markdown document on the constitutive progression of
Gödelian theories.

Let $T_0$ be a certified arithmetic stage. For every natural number $n$, the
executed progression constructs a stage $T_n$, a Gödelian production $G_n$,
and two computed terminals:

\begin{itemize}
  \item $t^{\mathrm{truth}}_n$, carrying the readout $\Truth(G_n)$;
  \item $t^{\mathrm{proof}}_n$, carrying the readout $\Proof_{T_n}(G_n)$.
\end{itemize}

The same executed production provides the witnesses

\[
  \Truth(G_n)
\]

and

\[
  \Proof_{T_n}(G_n) \longrightarrow \Empty.
\]

The following results then hold. In what follows, $C_n$ denotes the Gödelian
contract of the execution at stage $n$, and $q_A$ denotes the memory projection
of realization $A$.

\section{Positive operational grouping}

There exists an operational realization that computes a common terminal
memory for $t^{\mathrm{truth}}_n$ and $t^{\mathrm{proof}}_n$, without requiring
global exactness of their readouts.

The grouping of the terminal memories is therefore constructible. It
identifies neither the constitutive sources nor their readings.

\section{Universal separation under exactness}

For every memory type $M$ and every exact realization $A$ of the Gödelian
contract $C_n$,

\[
  \boxed{
    q_A\!\left(t^{\mathrm{truth}}_n\right)
    \neq
    q_A\!\left(t^{\mathrm{proof}}_n\right)
  }.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
  \boxed{
    \forall M,\;
    \forall A : \ExactRealization(C_n,M),\quad
    q_A\!\left(t^{\mathrm{truth}}_n\right)
    \neq
    q_A\!\left(t^{\mathrm{proof}}_n\right)
  }.
\]

No exact realization of the contract can therefore merge the memories of the
two computed terminals.

The two certified components of the same executed Gödelian witness --- truth
and unprovability of $G_n$ --- thus yield an additional consequence that
applies universally to the admissible exact representations of the regime.

\section{Constitutive progression}

The next stage is effectively constructed by the extension

\[
  T_{n+1} = T_n + \{G_n\}.
\]

This notation denotes the addition of the exact occurrence of $G_n$ to the
constituted history of $T_n$. At the new stage,

\[
  \Proof_{T_{n+1}}(G_n),
\]

while a new Gödelian production provides $G_{n+1}$ with

\[
  \Truth(G_{n+1})
\]

and

\[
  \Proof_{T_{n+1}}(G_{n+1}) \longrightarrow \Empty.
\]

The development also proves

\[
  G_n \neq G_{n+1}.
\]

Universal separation then applies again to the computed terminals of
$G_{n+1}$, relative to the contract of the new regime.

\section{Conclusion}

\begin{center}
  \fbox{%
    \begin{minipage}{0.82\linewidth}
      \centering
      At every effectively produced finite stage, the Gödelian production of
      that stage provides a new proof boundary and imposes, for its contract,
      a new universal separation obligation on every exact realization.
    \end{minipage}%
  }
\end{center}

The result therefore goes beyond the unprovability of $G_n$. It turns the
executed Gödelian witness into a universal constraint on every exact
realization covered by the contract of the stage.

At the same time, the positive construction of the operational grouping shows
exactly what remains possible when global readout exactness is not required.

The progression is finite for every requested natural height. The result
constructs neither an infinite tower, a limit union, a complete final theory,
nor a runtime spanning two stages.
