# Positive circularity and boundary transports

`RelationalPerimeter.Constitution` now exposes a positive circular presentation, a separate obstruction layer, exact adapters to the historical interface, and witness-preserving transports of selected closing-boundary data. The positive submodule can be imported independently of the obstructed historical machinery.

`PositiveCircularPresentation` receives the primitive sorts and families, the initial witnessed node, a successive spine with at least one non-closing position, and the distinguished closing junction. `EndpointBoundary` adds pole readings without assuming their separation. `CircularClosureObstruction` separately receives the loop/contraction interface and rejection. It proves endpoint separation under these additional assumptions.

The same positive example has identified pole readings in `Unit` and separated pole readings in `Bool`. Only the latter receives the obstruction. This separates positive closing data from rejection of identification. It does not produce a periodic generated history: raw repeated nodes and the strict cursors of the historical generator are different interfaces.

The historical `CircularPresentation` extends the positive structure. Its positive parent, endpoint readings and obstruction reconstruct it through `ofPositive`, with exact round trips for all three layers and for the complete historical object. The existing perimetral and computational proofs remain consumers of this enriched interface.

`ConstitutiveBoundary` selects the closing source/target and their compatibility fibre, the distinguished junction, and the initial difference/provenance fibre with its witness. `closingBoundary` extracts these values from the same positive presentation. `BoundaryCarrierTransport` supplies exact transports on five carriers. `BoundaryTransport` additionally preserves the three chosen indices and the two distinguished witnesses. Identity, inverse and composition calculate these agreements; the laws hold pointwise on all five carriers. Backward agreements are derived from the forward agreements and round trips.

The closing-witness swap example preserves the indices and exact carrier round trips but cannot lift to the enriched transport. Independent probes also reject a provenance-only swap. `EquippedFinalRole` stores the exact distinguished closing witness and reads its source, target and provenance from its indexing boundary. Enriched transports provide its exact forward and backward maps with preservation laws. The historical classification role `FinalRequirement` remains separate, as do generated occurrences.

`ClosingBoundaryShape` now omits the closing witness while retaining the selected indices and initial provenance. `PointedClosingBoundary B` supplies that witness. Exact round trips reconstruct the current `ConstitutiveBoundary`; witness and pointing types are exactly transported, while existence equivalences remain in `Prop`. Empty, `Unit` and `Bool` fibres distinguish existence, choice and role uniqueness over each fixed choice. See [the French account](frontiere-sans-jonction-choisie.fr.md).

`PositiveFormation` receives states, their witnessed nodes and admissible steps with compatibility readouts. `PositiveHistory` constructs finite composable paths; deployment preserves their endpoints, positions and selected readouts, with explicit endpoint reindexing for composition. Closing a positive path requires a separate witness. A chosen global continuation is optional, generic paths may return to a raw state, and deployment does not reconstruct arbitrary Step payloads. See [the positive generation account](formation-et-generation-positives.fr.md).

Lot 3 now adds [complete signature and formation transports](transports-signature-et-formation.fr.md): every relational fibre, mapped spines, exact structural positions and order, and a reconstructed formation retaining the same states and full step types. History transport commutes with composition and deployment; closing witnesses transport separately. This does not compare arbitrary pre-existing formations or transport pole functions and obstruction.

These morphisms preserve **selected closing-boundary data**, not every relation fibre, every pole reading, the entire spine, order or generated histories. Full signature and formation-operation transports, and a general constitutive quantity signature, remain open work. The full French account is in [Circularité positive et transports de frontière](circularite-positive-et-transports-frontiere.fr.md).

The implementation and examples use the pinned toolchain and constructive terminal audits. An independent AI referee checked the statements and separate transport models; human review remains pending. Project results retain tier T2.
